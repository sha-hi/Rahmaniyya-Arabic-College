About Us

History

Rahmaniyya Arabic College was founded with the vision of providing high-quality Islamic education in a modern environment. Located in the heart of Palakkad, our institution has been a beacon of knowledge for students from across the region.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a world-class center for Arabic and Islamic studies, fostering spiritual growth and intellectual excellence.

Mission: To provide students with a deep understanding of Islamic sciences, language, and culture, preparing them to guide society with wisdom and integrity.

Rahmaniyya Arabic College

Rahmaniyya Arabic College is a premier institution dedicated to preserving and spreading Islamic knowledge.
