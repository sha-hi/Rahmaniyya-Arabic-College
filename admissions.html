Admission Process

1. Application

Fill out the application form available at the college office or download it from our website.

2. Entrance Test

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an entrance examination focusing on basic Arabic and Islamic knowledge.

3. Interview

Successful candidates will have a personal interview with the faculty board.
